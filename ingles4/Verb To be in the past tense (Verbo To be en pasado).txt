Singular
1.ª persona --> I was ---> yo era/estaba
2.ª persona --> you were --> tú eras/estabas,usted era/estaba
3.ª persona --> he was --> él era/estaba
3.ª persona --> she was --> ella era/estaba
3.ª persona --> it was --> (eso) era/estaba

Plural
1.ª persona --> we were -->nosotros,éramos/estábamos
2.ª persona --> you were --> ustedes,eran/estaban
3.ª persona --> they were --> ellos eran/estaban

Warning #1
Advertencia #1
As shown in the table, all singular persons use "was" except for "you", which uses "were" for both singular and plural.
Como se muestra en la tabla, todas las personas singulares usan "was" con excepción de "you", que tanto para singular como plural usa "were".

For example/por ejemplo:

She was in the back and you were in the front of the photo.

Ella estaba en la parte de atrás y tú estabas en el frente de la foto.

Warning #2
Advertencia #2
In Spanish, the verb "to be" can refer to "ser" or “estar" depending on the context of the sentence.
En español, el verbo "to be" puede hacer referencia a "ser" o “estar" dependiendo del contexto de la oración.

For example/por ejemplo:

I was a student.

Yo era un estudiante.

I was in the classroom.

Yo estaba en el salón de clases.

He was young and handsome.--> Él era joven y guapo.
The puppies were so cute. -> Los cachorros eran tan lindos.
Sandra was in Colombia --> Sandra estaba en Colombia
We were abroad. --> Nosotros estábamos en el extranjero.
